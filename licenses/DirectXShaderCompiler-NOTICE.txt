DirectX Shader Compiler (DXC) - notice

The installer bundles dxcompiler.dll from the DirectX Shader Compiler, release v1.8.2407.
It is used by XenosRecomp to compile the HLSL generated from your own game's shaders to DXIL/SPIR-V on your PC.
